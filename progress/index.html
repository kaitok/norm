Help & support

Small steps.
Visible progress.

Track a number that matters to you, then keep it in view with a simple ring or bar on your Home Screen.

Create your first goal

  1. Open Progress and tap Add or Add progress.
  2. Enter a title, current value, target, and unit.
  3. Choose Ring or Bar under Design, then customize the background and text under Colors.
  4. Return to your goal whenever you want to raise or lower the current value. Counts are updated manually; a title such as “Days to go” does not create an automatic date countdown.

Add a Home Screen widget

  1. Create a goal in Progress first.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone or iPad Home Screen.
  3. Open the widget gallery using Edit → Add Widget or the + button, depending on your OS version.
  4. Search for Progress and add its small widget.
  5. Touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, and select the goal you want it to display.

You can add multiple widgets and assign a different goal to each one.

Automatic resets

In a goal’s Goal settings, choose None, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. Daily resets start at local midnight, weekly resets on Monday, and monthly resets on the first day of the month. An automatic reset sets the current value to zero.

Widget refresh timing is managed by iOS and iPadOS, so a change may not appear at the exact scheduled second. Open Progress to refresh the saved state.

Widget not updating?

  • Open Progress and check that the goal’s current and target values are correct.
  • Check the goal selected in Edit Widget.
  • Allow a little time for the system to refresh the widget. If necessary, remove and add the widget again.

Your data

Your goals and settings are stored locally on your device and shared with the Progress widget on that device. Progress does not provide its own cloud sync or account system.

To delete a goal, touch and hold its card in Progress and choose Delete. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
