Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Progress is developed by Kaito Kinjo. This policy describes the Progress iPhone and iPad app, its widget, and this support website.

Data collected by the app

Progress does not collect or transmit your goals, counts, or settings to the developer or third parties. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or account registration.

Local storage and deletion

Goal titles, current and target values, units, appearance settings, reset schedules, and related progress history are stored locally. The app and its widget share this storage using an Apple App Group on the same device.

You can delete individual goals by touching and holding a goal card and choosing Delete. You can remove the app and its locally stored data using your device’s app deletion controls; offloading the app may retain data. Device backups and restores are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. Progress does not operate a server-side account or its own cloud sync service.

Purchases and platform services

Apple handles App Store payments. The developer does not receive your payment card details through Progress. Apple may process purchase information, app diagnostics, or other platform data under its own policies and your device settings. Any sales or diagnostic reports made available by Apple are separate from data collection by the Progress app.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and investigate reported issues. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed for support, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Please do not send passwords, financial details, or sensitive goal information.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website hosting is separate from the app’s local data storage.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends personal information through a support request, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published.

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, contact Kaito Kinjo at kaitokinjo@gmail.com.
